# Setuvia - Hackathon Demo Script 🎬

**Target Time:** 3-4 minutes

## Prep
1. Ensure both `client` and `server` are running.
2. Open `http://localhost:5173` to the Login screen.
3. Have your `server/.env` configured (or rely on the deterministic mock fallback).

---

## 1. The Hook (Login Screen)
*Show the beautiful login screen.*
**Speaker:** "Customer support today is fractured. Customers repeat themselves across channels, and agents spend hours deciphering bills instead of solving problems. Meet Setuvia: an AI-native CX platform that fixes this."

## 2. Document Intelligence (Customer View)
*Click 'Simulate Customer'*
**Speaker:** "I am a frustrated hospital patient. I think I've been overcharged. I upload my bill into Setuvia's WhatsApp channel."
*Click 'Upload Bill-83921.pdf'*
**Speaker:** "Instantly, Setuvia's **BillSense module** extracts the data and runs deterministic mathematical checks against the hospital tariff policy. It doesn't just guess—it knows. It found 4 discrepancies totaling ₹6,800, including a duplicate charge and a tax error, and explains this to me in plain English."
*Click 'Get this resolved'*
**Speaker:** "I submit this, creating a case."

## 3. Omnichannel Memory (Customer View)
*Click 'Send Simulated Email'*
**Speaker:** "Later, the hospital emails an update. In a normal system, this creates a duplicate ticket. In Setuvia, our **Identity Resolver** and **Case Linker** instantly attach this email to the existing case timeline. No duplicates."

## 4. The Agent Copilot (Agent View)
*Log out. Click 'Login as Agent'. Select the newly created case.*
**Speaker:** "Now I'm the agent. Look at this workspace. I instantly see the **AI Summary**, so I don't have to read 20 messages. I see the full timeline—WhatsApp and Email merged perfectly. And at the bottom? An **AI Draft Reply**, ready to go."
*Edit the draft slightly, click 'Approve & Send'. Show it appear in the timeline.*
**Speaker:** "I review it, hit send, and the issue moves forward in seconds."

## 5. Rules Engine & CX Intelligence (Manager View)
*Log out. Click 'Login as Manager'. Click on 'Operations (Audit)'.*
**Speaker:** "Security is critical. Our **Immutable Audit Log** records everything. If an agent tries to approve a ₹6,800 refund..."
*Click 'Test Gate: Agent attempts ₹6800 refund'*
**Speaker:** "...our Rules Engine blocks it instantly, requiring manager approval."
*Click 'CX Intelligence' tab.*
**Speaker:** "Finally, solving single tickets isn't enough. Our **CX Intelligence** engine runs batch analysis on all resolved cases. It realized 14 patients had this exact same tax error, and recommends an automated fix to the core hospital management system. We aren't just closing tickets; we are fixing the business."

**Speaker:** "Setuvia. Better for the customer, effortless for the agent, insightful for the business. Thank you!"
